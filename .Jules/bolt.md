## 2025-05-27 - Deferred Loading via IntersectionObserver
**Learning:** While `React.lazy` and `Suspense` can split bundles for below-the-fold components, unconditional rendering triggers immediate network fetches. To truly defer loading until components enter the viewport and prevent waterfall blocking, they must be conditionally rendered using an Intersection Observer wrapper. Furthermore, when applying lazy loading wrappers to sections targeted by anchor links, the wrapper must be placed *inside* the structural element carrying the `id` attribute (e.g., `<section id='Productos'>`), otherwise the anchor is removed from the DOM before it loads, breaking single-page navigation.
**Action:** Always use an Intersection Observer alongside `React.lazy` to defer loading of below-the-fold components until they approach the viewport, providing a `minHeight` placeholder to prevent CLS. Ensure lazy loading wrappers are placed inside anchor-targeted IDs, not wrapping them.
