## 2025-02-12 - Intersection Observer CLS with React Lazy Loading
**Learning:** When combining `IntersectionObserver` wrappers with `React.lazy` and `Suspense` for below-the-fold components, removing the wrapper's `minHeight` immediately upon intersection causes a severe Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) regression. The component isn't instantly ready due to the network fetch required by `Suspense`. During this loading window, the container collapses to 0px, causing the page structure below the viewport to jump wildly.
**Action:** Always maintain the `minHeight` prop permanently on lazy-loading container wrappers, or keep it applied until a callback confirms the child component has fully rendered and mounted to the DOM. Placing the wrapper *inside* the layout elements with `id` targets ensures anchor links continue to function correctly.
