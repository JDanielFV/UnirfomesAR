## 2024-05-31 - Safe IntersectionObserver with Suspense
**Learning:** When implementing `LazySection` wrappers with `IntersectionObserver` and `React.lazy`, do not dynamically change the wrapper's `minHeight` to 'auto' immediately upon intersection. The network fetch triggered by `Suspense` takes time, and removing the minimum height prematurely collapses the container to 0px, causing a severe Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) regression. Furthermore, ensuring that the wrapper is placed *inside* the structural element carrying the `id` attribute (e.g., `<section id='Productos'>`) is crucial; wrapping the outer element removes the anchor from the DOM before it loads, breaking single-page navigation.
**Action:** Maintain the `minHeight` permanently or until the child component has fully rendered, and always wrap the inner content rather than the container with the `id` attribute.
