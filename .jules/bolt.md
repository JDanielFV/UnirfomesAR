## 2025-05-18 - Viewport-based lazy loading with Intersection Observer and Suspense
**Learning:** Using `React.lazy()` and `<Suspense>` at the root level of an app triggers an immediate network fetch for all components, causing a waterfall and defeating the purpose of lazy loading. To truly defer loading for below-the-fold content, the component must be wrapped in an `IntersectionObserver` that only renders it when visible. Furthermore, dynamically changing the minHeight of this wrapper immediately upon intersection collapses the container, causing a severe Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) regression while the lazy chunk is fetched.
**Action:** Always wrap below-the-fold components in an Intersection Observer (like `LazySection`) placed *inside* structural `<section>` elements (to maintain anchor navigation). Include a persistent `minHeight` prop on the wrapper to maintain layout and prevent CLS until the Suspense fetch completes.
