## 2024-05-24 - Accessible Custom Modal Overlays
**Learning:** When implementing custom modal overlays, basic rendering and styling are not enough. Modals without keyboard dismissal (Escape key) and outside-click handlers trap users, causing poor UX. Missing ARIA roles and labels (like `role="dialog"` and `aria-modal="true"`) make the modal invisible or confusing to screen readers, violating accessibility standards.
**Action:** Always ensure accessibility and proper UX by attaching a global 'keydown' event listener for 'Escape' key dismissal, adding 'role="dialog"' and 'aria-modal="true"' attributes to the modal container, and implementing an outside-click handler by verifying 'e.target === e.currentTarget' on the overlay wrapper. Ensure interactive elements like close buttons have descriptive 'aria-label' attributes.
